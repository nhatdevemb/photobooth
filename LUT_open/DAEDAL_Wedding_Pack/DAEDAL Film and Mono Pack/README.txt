DAEDAL Film and Mono Pack — free creative LUTs by DAEDAL

WHAT'S INSIDE
  Creative LUTs (.cube, 33-point). License: CC0 — free for any use, see LICENSE.txt.

IMPORTANT: these are CREATIVE LUTs for Rec.709 footage.
  Apply them AFTER your footage is converted/corrected to Rec.709.
  Shooting Log (V-Log, S-Log3, C-Log, D-Log...)? First apply your camera's
  official Log-to-Rec.709 LUT, then one of these on top.

PREMIERE PRO
  Lumetri Color → Creative → Look → Browse… → pick a .cube file.
  Use "Intensity" to dial it in (most looks are best at 60–90%).
  Tip: put Lumetri on an Adjustment Layer to grade the whole timeline at once.

DAVINCI RESOLVE
  Project Settings → Color Management → Lookup Tables → Open LUT Folder,
  copy the .cube files there, click "Update Lists". Then in the Color page:
  right-click a node → LUT → pick the look. Use Key Output Gain to reduce strength.

FINAL CUT PRO
  Effects → Color → Custom LUT → Choose Custom LUT… → pick a .cube file.

Expose and white-balance first: LUTs look as good as the footage under them.
